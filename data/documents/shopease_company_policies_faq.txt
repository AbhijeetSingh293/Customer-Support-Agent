1. ABOUT SHOPEASE
ShopEase is a fictional online shopping platform offering electronics, home and kitchen products,
fashion, books, and everyday accessories. Customers can place orders through the ShopEase website
or mobile application. Orders are subject to product availability and seller confirmation.

2. ORDER PLACEMENT AND CONFIRMATION
- After placing an order, the customer receives an order confirmation by email or SMS.
- An order is considered confirmed when its status changes to "Confirmed" in the customer account.
- Customers should check the delivery address and phone number immediately after placing an order.
- If an order is still in "Placed" status, the customer may request cancellation.
- Orders that have entered "Shipped" status generally cannot be cancelled through self-service.
- Product prices and availability may change before an order is confirmed.

3. SHIPPING AND DELIVERY
- Standard delivery usually takes 3–7 business days after order confirmation.
- Metro-city deliveries may arrive sooner; remote locations may require additional time.
- Express delivery, when available, usually takes 1–3 business days.
- Delivery estimates are not guarantees and may be affected by weather, public holidays,
  courier delays, or incorrect address information.
- A tracking link is shared once the order is handed to the courier.
- If tracking has not updated for 48 hours, the customer should contact support.
- If a package is marked delivered but has not been received, the customer should check with
  household members, reception, or the building security desk, then contact support within
  48 hours of the delivery update.

4. RETURNS
- Eligible products can be returned within 7 days of delivery.
- The product must be unused, undamaged, and returned with original packaging, tags,
  accessories, manuals, and included items.
- Some categories may be non-returnable for hygiene, safety, or customization reasons.
- Products marked "Non-returnable" on the product page are not eligible for a change-of-mind return.
- To request a return, open My Orders, select the relevant order, choose "Return item",
  select a reason, and submit the request.
- ShopEase may arrange a pickup or provide return instructions depending on the delivery PIN code.
- Returned products may be inspected before a refund is approved.
- A return request does not guarantee approval; the item must meet the return conditions.

5. DAMAGED, DEFECTIVE, OR WRONG ITEMS
- Customers should report a damaged, defective, or incorrect item within 48 hours of delivery.
- Include clear photos of the item, packaging, shipping label, and any visible damage.
- Keep the product and packaging until the support team provides instructions.
- After verification, ShopEase may offer a replacement, refund, or another suitable resolution.
- Do not send an item to an address that has not been provided by the official support team.

6. REFUNDS
- Refunds are initiated after the return is approved and any required inspection is completed.
- After initiation, refunds to the original payment method usually take 5–7 business days.
- UPI and eligible wallet refunds may be completed sooner, depending on the payment provider.
- For Cash on Delivery orders, the customer may be asked to provide valid bank or UPI details
  through the secure account flow.
- Shipping fees may not be refundable for change-of-mind returns. They may be refunded when
  the return is due to a verified ShopEase error, damaged item, or incorrect item.
- If the expected refund period has passed, the customer should share the order ID with support.
- Never ask a customer to share a password, OTP, CVV, or full card number to process a refund.

7. CANCELLATIONS
- Customers can request cancellation before an order is shipped.
- Once shipped, cancellation may no longer be available; the customer can check return eligibility
  after delivery.
- If ShopEase cancels an order because of stock or payment issues, the customer will be notified
  and any captured payment will be refunded.
- Refund timelines for cancelled prepaid orders are usually 5–7 business days after initiation.

8. WARRANTY AND SERVICE
- Manufacturer warranty applies only when it is explicitly stated on the product page or warranty
  card supplied with the product.
- Warranty duration and coverage vary by brand and product.
- Warranty generally covers manufacturing defects under normal use; accidental damage, liquid
  damage, misuse, and unauthorized repairs may be excluded.
- Customers should retain the invoice and warranty card.
- For warranty claims, contact the product manufacturer or an authorized service center when
  the product documentation directs the customer to do so.
- ShopEase support can help locate order and invoice information but cannot override a
  manufacturer's warranty decision.

9. PAYMENTS
- Available payment methods may include UPI, debit cards, credit cards, net banking, and
  Cash on Delivery for eligible PIN codes and orders.
- An order is confirmed only after the payment provider reports a successful payment, where
  prepaid payment is required.
- If money is debited but the order is not confirmed, wait up to 30 minutes for the status to update.
  If it remains unsuccessful, contact support with the transaction reference.
- Never share an OTP, UPI PIN, CVV, password, or complete card details with anyone claiming
  to be ShopEase support.

10. ACCOUNT AND PRIVACY
- Customers should use the email address or phone number registered to their ShopEase account
  when contacting support.
- Customers are responsible for keeping their passwords and OTPs confidential.
- Support agents should verify account ownership before discussing order-specific information.
- Only the minimum information needed to resolve a support request should be collected.
- Do not expose one customer's order, address, phone number, or payment details to another customer.

12. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Q: How can I track my order?
A: Sign in, open My Orders, select the order, and choose Track Order. Tracking becomes available
   after the courier receives the package.

Q: How long does delivery take?
A: Standard delivery usually takes 3–7 business days after confirmation. The estimate can vary
   by delivery location and courier conditions.

Q: Can I cancel an order?
A: You can request cancellation before the order is shipped. For shipped orders, check the
   return policy after delivery.

Q: What is the return window?
A: Eligible items can generally be returned within 7 days of delivery, subject to product
   condition and category restrictions.

Q: My item arrived damaged. What should I do?
A: Report the issue within 48 hours of delivery. Provide photos of the item and packaging,
   and keep the product until support gives instructions.

Q: When will I receive my refund?
A: Once a refund is initiated, it usually takes 5–7 business days to appear in the original
   payment method. Payment-provider timelines may vary.

Q: Is every product covered by a warranty?
A: No. Warranty coverage depends on the product and manufacturer. Check the product page,
   invoice, and warranty card.

Q: What should I do if payment was deducted but my order was not created?
A: Wait up to 30 minutes for the order status to update. If it does not, contact support with
   the transaction reference. Never share your OTP or UPI PIN.

Q: Can I change my delivery address after ordering?
A: Contact support immediately. Address changes may only be possible before the order is
   processed or shipped.

Q: The tracking page says delivered, but I did not receive the parcel. What now?
A: Check with household members, reception, or building security. If it is still missing,
   contact support within 48 hours of the delivery update.

13. GUIDANCE FOR THE AI CUSTOMER SUPPORT ASSISTANT
- Use these documents as general policy guidance, not as proof that a specific order is eligible.
- For order status, refund status, and customer-specific details, use the authorized backend tools
  and database records. Do not guess or invent order information.
- Enforce the authenticated customer's identity on the backend; never trust a customer ID
  supplied by the language model or typed into a prompt.
- If a policy is unclear, a product is excluded, or the customer disputes a decision, escalate
  to a human support agent.
- Do not promise a refund, replacement, or delivery date before the relevant system confirms it.
- Do not request passwords, OTPs, CVVs, UPI PINs, or full payment-card numbers.
